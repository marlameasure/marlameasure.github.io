THE CONVERSION ASSUMPTION

How to convert Murabba to acres

This converter uses 1 Murabba = 25 acres, a convention found in Punjab agricultural land references. Multiply Murabba by 25 to get acres, or divide acres by 25 to get Murabba. Check your local land record for the convention that applies to your holding.

QUICK ANSWERS

Murabba conversion questions

About the agricultural-unit convention used here.

How many acres are in one Murabba?

This calculator uses 25 acres per Murabba, a common Punjab agricultural convention. Confirm the definition in the relevant land records.

How many kanal are in one Murabba?

At 25 acres per Murabba and the default 272.25 sq ft marla standard, one Murabba equals 200 kanal. The result changes with the selected marla standard because kanal area is based on 20 marla.

How many marla are in one Murabba?

At the default 272.25 sq ft per marla, 25 acres equal 4,000 marla. Select the marla standard used by your records to see a different equivalent if needed.

Does this converter confirm the size in my land record?

No. It applies the stated conversion convention to the number you enter. Check the relevant land record or authority for the official area and local unit definitions.
