Convert marla, square feet and kanal

All area conversions below use the same selected marla size. This keeps the calculator, examples and reference table consistent.

A

Square feet to marla

Divide square feet by the chosen square feet per marla.

marla = square feet ÷ marla sizeFor 1,000 sq ft at 250 sq ft per marla: 1,000 ÷ 250 = 4 marla.
B

Marla to square feet

Multiply marla by the chosen square feet per marla.

square feet = marla × marla sizeFor 5 marla at 225 sq ft per marla: 5 × 225 = 1,125 sq ft.

QUICK ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions

Practical notes on land area conversions in Pakistan.

How many square feet are in one marla in Pakistan?

It depends on the property convention. This calculator supports 225, 250 and 272.25 sq ft per marla, plus a custom value. Check the applicable standard in the scheme’s documents or your property records.

How do I convert square feet to marla?

Divide the area in square feet by the chosen square feet per marla. For example, 1,250 sq ft is 5 marla when using 250 sq ft per marla.

How many marla are in one kanal?

This calculator uses 20 marla per kanal. One kanal is therefore 20 marla regardless of which square-foot marla size you select.

How many square feet are in 5 marla?

It depends on the selected standard: 1,125 sq ft at 225 sq ft per marla, 1,250 sq ft at 250, or 1,361.25 sq ft at 272.25.

How do I calculate marla from plot length and width?

Multiply length by width to find the rectangular area. If dimensions are in feet, divide the result in square feet by the selected marla size. If dimensions are in metres, convert the area to square feet first; the dimensions mode does this automatically.

Why do different calculators show different marla results?

They may use different square-foot definitions for a marla. Select the value relevant to your property and check the convention used by any calculator you compare with.

Can I use a custom marla size?

Yes. Select Custom size and enter a positive square-foot value per marla. That value is used for all marla and kanal area calculations on the page.

Does this calculator verify a plot’s official area?

No. It converts values you enter and calculates area from dimensions. It does not check property documents, boundaries, legal status or official records.
